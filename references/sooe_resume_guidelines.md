# Central State-Owned Enterprise (央国企) Resume Guidelines

This guide details specific requirements, review habits, and content formatting principles essential for Central Enterprises and State-Owned Enterprises (央企、国企、事业单位及体制内招聘).

## 1. Non-Negotiable Candidate Metadata

Unlike general internet tech companies, state-owned enterprise HR and evaluation committees heavily emphasize political qualification, hometown origins, and educational pedigree:

1. **政治面貌 (Political Affiliation)**:
   - Must be prominently placed: `中共党员` (Communist Party Member), `共青团员` (Communist Youth League Member), or `群众`.
2. **籍贯与现居地 (Origin & Residence)**:
   - `籍贯：省份+地级市` (e.g., 安徽芜湖). State-owned enterprises consider local stability and long-term retention.
   - `现居城市：地级市` (e.g., 芜湖市).
3. **出生年月与民族 (Birth & Ethnicity)**:
   - Standard format: `YYYY.MM` (e.g., 2001.10).
   - Ethnicity: `汉族` or specific minority group.
4. **学业时间轴与统招全日制标识 (Education Timeline)**:
   - Always list both Master's and Bachelor's degrees with exact start and projected graduation dates (e.g., `2023.09 - 2026.12（预计毕业）`).

## 2. Phrasing Principles: Restraint, Stability & Closure

- **Avoid Hollow Buzzwords**: Avoid pure internet jargon (e.g., 赋能、闭环抓手、打法、心智沉淀) without concrete engineering actions.
- **Emphasize Engineering Closure & Stability**:
  - Highlight full-stack ownership: from requirements gathering, database modeling, and backend architecture to Linux deployment and automated monitoring.
  - State clearly: willingness to work on frontlines, cross-functional teamwork, stability, and organizational discipline.
- **Quantifiable Results & Real-World Deployments**:
  - Highlight municipal or community implementations (e.g., "已在杭州多个社区常态化落地使用").
  - Quantify latency reduction, concurrency conflict elimination, and failure recovery metrics.

## 3. Strict 1-Page A4 Budgeting

- Most state-owned enterprise recruitment interviewers review printed physical paper resumes during panel interviews.
- **Never allow 2nd page spillover**: A second page with 2-3 orphan lines signals poor editing control.
- Ensure bottom margins are balanced, eliminating excessive blank voids while maintaining natural breathing room.
